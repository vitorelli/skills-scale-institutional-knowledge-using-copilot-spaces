# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owners

### Role Summary
Product Owners translate business goals into clear priorities and outcomes. They act as the primary connection between customer or stakeholder needs and the delivery team, ensuring that the most valuable work is planned and delivered first.

### Responsibilities
- Define and prioritize work based on business value and customer need
- Clarify scope, acceptance criteria, and trade-offs for the team
- Review progress against goals and adjust priorities when circumstances change
- Partner with Project Managers to ensure delivery milestones align with business needs
- Represent stakeholder interests during planning, review, and release decisions

### Goals
- Maximize business value and user impact
- Create clear priorities for the team
- Reduce ambiguity and rework by aligning expectations early

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and prioritization sessions
- Weekly product alignment reviews with the Project Manager and stakeholders
- Acceptance criteria discussions during planning and demos

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on roadmap timing, risks, and dependency management
- Collaborates with Developers and Technical Leads on feasibility, sequencing, and quality expectations
- Provides direction to the team while staying aligned with stakeholder needs and broader product strategy

---

## Delivery Managers

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers coordinate the execution of work across teams and dependencies. They focus on flow, capacity, and operational health so that the plan remains realistic and the team can deliver with fewer disruptions.

### Responsibilities
- Monitor workstreams, blockers, and dependencies across teams
- Help maintain delivery cadence and forecast risks to milestones
- Support capacity planning and resource coordination
- Escalate issues that require stakeholder or cross-functional intervention
- Coordinate communication between project leadership, engineering, and operational partners

### Goals
- Improve execution consistency and team throughput
- Ensure dependencies are visible and managed early
- Keep delivery aligned with project commitments and constraints

### Typical Communication
- Cross-team dependency reviews and operating rhythm meetings
- Delivery dashboards and milestone check-ins
- Status updates to Project Managers and leadership stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to track timelines, risks, and operational readiness
- Coordinates with Developers and Technical Leads to surface issues that might affect delivery dates or quality
- Partners with Product Owners to understand priority trade-offs and resource implications

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide engineering decisions and technical direction. They translate product intent into implementation approaches, uphold standards, and ensure that delivery decisions are technically sound and sustainable.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach, architecture constraints, and engineering standards
- Review design trade-offs and ensure alignment with platform and quality goals
- Guide implementation quality through design reviews and technical mentoring
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation strategies
- Support Developers in resolving complex problems and ensuring sustainable delivery

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that are maintainable, secure, and scalable
- Balance technical quality with delivery speed and business priorities
- Reduce avoidable defects and technical rework

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design review sessions
- Engineering planning and risk discussions
- Technical dependency updates and implementation guidance

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Owners to confirm feasibility, sequencing, and trade-offs
- Works with Project Managers to understand schedule impact and risks associated with technical choices
- Supports Developers by clarifying technical decisions while remaining aligned with team commitments and quality standards

---

## Risk and Communication Leads

### Role Summary
Risk and Communication Leads ensure that project stakeholders understand priorities, risks, and decisions. They help maintain visibility, escalate issues early, and support a coordinated narrative across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Track project risks, dependencies, and escalation triggers
- Maintain communication plans for stakeholders, teams, and leadership
- Surface emerging issues before they become blockers
- Coordinate messaging across project milestones, status updates, and change communication
- Support decision-making by ensuring relevant context reaches the right audiences

### Goals
- Improve visibility and proactive issue management
- Reduce confusion and delays caused by unclear communications
- Strengthen stakeholder confidence through timely, consistent updates

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews and stakeholder briefings
- Strategic communications, status updates, and escalation notices
- Dependencies and decision logs shared with project leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to identify, prioritize, and communicate risks
- Supports Product Owners in aligning stakeholder expectations with delivery realities
- Provides cross-functional visibility that helps Developers, Technical Leads, and leadership make informed decisions

---

## Business Analysts / Engagement Partners

### Role Summary
Business Analysts and Engagement Partners turn stakeholder needs into clear requirements and coordination points. They support alignment between business intent, user needs, and operational execution.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and clarify stakeholder requirements and business context
- Translate needs into actionable requirements, workflows, and acceptance criteria
- Identify gaps or inconsistencies between stakeholder expectations and delivery plans
- Support change management and stakeholder alignment through project phases
- Facilitate cross-functional understanding of priorities and constraints

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder needs are captured accurately and translated into execution-ready work
- Reduce misunderstandings between business and delivery teams
- Improve decision quality through well-defined requirements and context

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Alignment meetings with Product Owners, Project Managers, and leadership
- Iterative feedback loops during validation and release readiness reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Owners to refine priorities and clarify business value
- Partners with Project Managers to translate stakeholder needs into realistic plans and milestones
- Supports Developers and Technical Leads by turning ambiguous requirements into clear decisions and testable outcomes

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These role definitions help teams understand accountability, decision rights, and collaboration patterns across project execution.
